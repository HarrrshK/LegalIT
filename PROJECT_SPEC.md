# LegalIt Product Specification

LegalIt is one role-aware legal operations platform for Indian clients, legal professionals, and administrators. It must provide secure case collaboration, lawyer discovery and consultations, practice management, document handling, legal research, sourced AI answers, legal updates, and operational administration.

## Product boundaries

- The platform provides workflow software and legal information. It does not claim official court tracking without a verified integration.
- AI output is informational, source constrained, and never a substitute for professional judgment.
- Lawyer verification is an explicit administrator decision. Registration alone never grants a verified badge.
- Client visibility is granted per case and per document. Internal lawyer notes and research remain private by default.
- Empty states are real. The application does not fabricate lawyers, cases, news, credentials, sources, or citations.

## Initial release acceptance

The definition of done in the master project brief is authoritative. Work proceeds through the milestones recorded in `PROJECT_STATUS.md`; incomplete features must remain labeled as incomplete.
